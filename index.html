Privacy Policy – Tia in the Jungle

Last updated: November 2026

"Tia in the Jungle" is an offline jigsaw puzzle game for children and families.

  • The game does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information.
  • The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no in-app purchases, and it does not need an internet connection.
  • Your progress (solved puzzles, stars, language and sound settings) is saved only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game.
